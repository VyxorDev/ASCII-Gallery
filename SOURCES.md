This registry tracks each exhibit's original source, creator, and permission status. A missing source or unverified license does **not** mean the image is free to use.

## Before new exhibits are published

1. Record the direct source image URL (not only a search-results link) and the creator's name, if known.
2. Check the actual license or written permission for **adapting the image** and **publishing the resulting PNG** on a public gallery. A free download or a credit line is not a substitute for permission.
3. Preserve attribution and license notices. Follow noncommercial and share-alike conditions when relevant. Works with **NoDerivatives (ND)** terms generally cannot have adapted versions distributed under that license.
4. If rights cannot be established, **hold the image for review** or replace the source with an original, public-domain, CC0, or suitably licensed image.
5. Only apply a license to material you own or have authority to license. Source rights remain with their original owners.
